Messages and enquiries

If you use the contact form or chat, the name and email address you provide, your message and its category are stored on this portfolio's server so Anuj can review and reply. An email address is optional in quick chat, but a reply may not be possible without one.

The form includes basic spam protection. Please do not include passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal data in a message.

Website feedback

Your required 1–5 star rating and any optional suggestion are stored in a private admin inbox. A name is optional. Ratings and suggestions are not published as testimonials on the public website.

Optional analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is used only when a measurement ID has been configured for this website and you choose to accept analytics. Until you accept, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Analytics may use cookies and process technical usage information under Google's privacy terms.

You can accept or decline analytics when the consent banner appears. You may also change your choice at any time:

If analytics is not configured, no analytics script or consent banner is enabled.

Storage, access and deletion

Submitted messages and feedback are kept in the website database until the site owner deletes them. They are accessible to the site owner through the password-protected admin panel, not through the public website.

Downloadable resume

The downloadable resume is shared at the site owner's request. It includes the name, email, phone number and location printed in the supplied resume. Please see Anuj's resume before contacting him if you need those details.

Certificates

Selected course and training certificates are publicly viewable from the portfolio at the site owner's request. Academic marksheets and other personal documents are not published.

Third-party services

Google Analytics is contacted only after consent and only if configured. The site also loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs to display its design. Your web host may process technical connection details when you visit this site.

Questions or requests

To ask about a message you sent or request its removal, use the contact form and include the email address you used, if available.
