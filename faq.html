Curious about a project, looking to collaborate or just saying hello? Start here.

Who is Anuj Kumar?

Anuj is a Bachelor of Computer Applications student building practical skills in web development and software engineering.

Can I ask about a project or collaboration?

Yes. Choose a project, collaboration or general enquiry on the contact form, or send a quick note through the chat button on the homepage. Your message goes directly into Anuj's private portfolio inbox.

Which parts of the portfolio can I try?

The calculator and login screen are interactive front-end learning demos. The login demo does not create accounts or save passwords.

How long does a reply take?

Anuj will reply when available. If you would like an email response, include a valid email address in your enquiry or chat message.

Where does my website feedback go?

Ratings and suggestions are sent to the private portfolio admin panel and are not displayed publicly as testimonials. A name is optional; a rating is required.

Does this website collect analytics or contact details?

Contact and chat messages are stored privately so Anuj can respond. Google Analytics only loads if configured and you explicitly opt in. See the privacy policy for details.

Send a message and Anuj will get back to you when available.
